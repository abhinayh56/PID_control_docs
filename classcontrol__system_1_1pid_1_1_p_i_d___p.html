Detailed Description

template<typename T>
class control_system::pid::PID_P< T >

PID controller with configurable derivative filtering and feed-forward.

The PID_P class implements a discrete-time PID controller with configurable proportional, integral, and derivative gains.

The derivative contribution can optionally be passed through a first-order low-pass filter. A feed-forward contribution can also be added to the controller output.

The integral contribution is constrained by the configured integral limit, while the final controller output is constrained by the configured output limit.
